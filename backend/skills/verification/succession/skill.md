# Skill: Succession and inheritance

Load when an owner died and the property passed to heirs, by will or by intestate succession.

> Draft. To be confirmed and extended by the product owner's lawyer instructions.

## How it is proved
- Inheritance without a will is a transfer by law. **It needs no registered deed.** Add it to the chain as a "Succession" link from the deceased to the heirs.
- A death certificate and a family or surviving-members certificate are documents that report the succession. Insert the Succession link; never mark a break for it. If the heirship document is unclear or does not name every seller, judge that in the succession's verdict (concern or missing document) and request the one document that settles it.
- **Intestate succession** is proved by:
  - the death certificate (who died, and when);
  - who the legal heirs are: a family tree, surviving family members certificate, legal heir certificate or succession certificate;
  - the mutation (or khata) entry in the heirs' names.
- **Succession under a will** is proved by the will, its proper execution, and probate or letters of administration where the law requires them, followed by mutation.

## Checks
- The death is before the heirs' transfer.
- Every heir named in the heirship document joins the later transfer, or releases their share by a registered release deed.
- A minor heir's share is sold only with court permission (see `minor_property`).
- Heirs are never inferred from similar names.

## Requisitions (only what is missing)
- Death certificate, if not uploaded.
- A heirship document (family tree or legal heir certificate), if not uploaded.
- The mutation in the heirs' names, if the revenue records do not show it.
- A registered release deed from each heir who did not join the later transfer.
- For a will: the will, and probate where required.
