# Skill: Succession and inheritance

Load when an owner died and the property passed to heirs, by will or by intestate succession.

> Draft. To be confirmed and extended by the product owner's lawyer instructions.

## Checks
- Death of the owner and its date, from a death certificate or a stated recital.
- Who the legal heirs are, from a family tree, legal heir certificate, succession certificate or probate.
- Every heir joins any later transfer, or releases their share by a registered release deed.
- A will: its execution, and probate where required.
- Mutation in the heirs' names.

## Red flags
An heir who did not join or release; a will without probate where needed; heirs inferred from similar names.

## Requisitions
Death certificate, family tree or legal heir certificate, release deeds, probate.
