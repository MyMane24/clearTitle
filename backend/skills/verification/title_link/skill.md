# Skill: Verify one transfer

Load for every chain link: the standard checks for a sale, gift or other transfer instrument.

> Draft. To be confirmed and extended by the product owner's lawyer instructions.

## Checks
- **Vendor's title:** the transferors acquired this property in the previous link (or a recital explains how), and held the whole interest they convey.
- **Parties and capacity:** every co-owner joins; anyone signing for another holds a valid authority (see `gpa_and_capacity`); a minor's share needs court permission (see `minor_property`).
- **Schedule and extent:** survey or site number, extent and boundaries match the previous link and the revenue records; any difference is explained.
- **Execution and registration:** executed and registered at the proper sub-registrar, with registration number and date.
- **Stamp duty:** paid and endorsed.
- **Consideration:** stated and its payment recited.
- **Mutation:** the revenue records were updated after the transfer (see `revenue_records`).

## Evidence
The transfer's own instrument proves it. A history entry or a later recital points to it but does not replace it.

## Red flags
Missing co-owner; extent larger than the vendor held; unregistered or unstamped instrument; power of attorney sale without the power of attorney; vendor not the previous acquirer.

## Requisitions
The instrument itself when absent; the power of attorney, court permission or death certificate when the transfer depends on one.
