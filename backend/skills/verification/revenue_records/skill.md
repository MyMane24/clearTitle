# Skill: Revenue records

Load once per case to check that the RTC and mutation records follow the title.

> Draft. To be confirmed and extended by the product owner's lawyer instructions.

## Checks
- A mutation entry after each transfer, naming the acquirer.
- The current RTC holder is the current owner.
- Survey and hissa numbers and extents agree across RTC, mutation and deeds; sub-divisions are recorded.
- Tenure, grant or government land entries.

## Red flags
RTC still in a previous owner's name; extent mismatch; government or grant land entries.

## Missing documents (list each one that is not uploaded)
Mutation register extract, current RTC, survey sketch or hissa tippani.
