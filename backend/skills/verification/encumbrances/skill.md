# Skill: Encumbrances

Load once per case to review mortgages, charges, attachments, leases and agreements.

> Draft. To be confirmed and extended by the product owner's lawyer instructions.

## Checks
- A mortgage, deposit of title deeds or charge of an earlier owner was released before the property passed to the next owner.
- A charge that still subsists against the present owner is disclosed in the encumbrances note; it is not a concern and no release is asked for.
- Attachments, court orders, lis pendens and pending litigation.
- Agreements of sale, leases or rights still subsisting.
- RTC column 11 (rights and liabilities) entries such as loans.
- The encumbrance certificate covers the whole period and the full property description.

## Red flags
A mortgage or charge left unreleased when a later transfer passed the property; an attachment or pending case; an encumbrance certificate that does not cover the period or the property.

## Missing documents (list each one that is not uploaded)
Release deed or bank no-dues letter for a mortgage left unreleased mid-chain, listed on the transfer that passed the property; encumbrance certificate for the missing period; court records.
