# Skill: Power of attorney and capacity

Load when a party signs through a power of attorney, or as a director, partner, trustee or other representative.

> Draft. To be confirmed and extended by the product owner's lawyer instructions.

## Checks
- The power of attorney exists, is registered where required, authorises this sale of this property, and was in force on the date of execution.
- For a company, firm or trust: the resolution or constitution authorising the signatory.
- The principal, not the attorney, held title.

## Red flags
Sale by an attorney without the power of attorney; a power of attorney sale treated as a transfer of title; a principal who died before the sale.

## Requisitions
The power of attorney, board resolution, partnership or trust deed.
