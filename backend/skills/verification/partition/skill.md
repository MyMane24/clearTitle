# Skill: Partition and family settlement

Load when a partition or family settlement allotted the target property, or when one is recited or listed in a history document.

> Draft. To be confirmed and extended by the product owner's lawyer instructions.

## Checks
- The partition instrument is registered (or an oral partition is followed by mutation and is accepted only as stated).
- The schedule allotted to the transferor matches the target property.
- All coparceners joined, including any minors through a guardian.
- Mutation followed the partition.

## Red flags
A coparcener left out; the target parcel allotted to someone else; an unregistered partition deed relied on as title.

## Requisitions
The partition deed, its schedule, and the mutation entry.
