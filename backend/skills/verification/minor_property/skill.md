# Skill: Minors' interests

Load when a minor owns, inherits or joins in transferring any share of the property.

> Draft. To be confirmed and extended by the product owner's lawyer instructions.

## Checks
- A guardian sold or released a minor's share only with court permission.
- Who signed for the minor and in what capacity.

## Red flags
A minor's share sold without court permission; a minor omitted from a family transfer.

## Missing documents (list each one that is not uploaded)
The court permission order; the guardianship order.
