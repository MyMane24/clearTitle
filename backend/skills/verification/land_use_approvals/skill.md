# Skill: Land use and approvals

Load when the property is or was agricultural land, a layout site, or a building.

> Draft. To be confirmed and extended by the product owner's lawyer instructions.

## Checks
- Conversion of agricultural land to non-agricultural use (conversion order) and its conditions.
- Restrictions on granted land (PTCL), inam or other non-alienation conditions.
- Khata and property tax in the owner's name.
- Approved layout or building plan, and completion or occupancy certificate for a building.

## Red flags
Agricultural land sold for non-agricultural use without conversion; granted land sold without permission; building without approval.

## Requisitions
Conversion order, khata extract and tax receipts, approved plan, completion certificate.
