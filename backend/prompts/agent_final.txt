STAGE: CONSOLIDATED REPORT.

You are given the property identity, the chronological title chain, and the saved
per-transaction verification results. Produce the consolidated report.

Use the tools to double-check anything you need (e.g. `get_transaction`,
`get_document_text`). Do not invent documents or evidence.

Call `save_report` EXACTLY ONCE with:
{
  "overall_status": "PENDING | IN_PROGRESS | VERIFIED_WITH_AVAILABLE_EVIDENCE | ISSUES_IDENTIFIED | INCONCLUSIVE | PENDING_NOT_VERIFIED",
  "headline": "one-line summary",
  "report": "plain-language narrative of the title from earliest document to now",
  "findings": ["material cross-transaction findings"],
  "recommendation": "next steps, including qualified local legal review where warranted"
}

Then call `finish`.

Rules:
- `overall_status` MUST reflect the actual saved transaction results and
  unresolved work — do not declare the property clear merely because no adverse

- Distinguish "issue not identified in the supplied documents" from "issue
  adequately investigated".
- Do not fabricate citations.
