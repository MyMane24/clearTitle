PROPERTY EVOLUTION AND CHAIN CONTINUITY
Treat the title chain as transfers of a specific interest in an evolving property, not as a list of deeds presumed to concern an unchanged asset. For example, agricultural land may later be converted to non-agricultural use, subdivided or developed into a project, and a particular flat may then be conveyed. Verify the documentary bridge from parent survey parcel to converted/layout/project land and from project to the exact unit and its undivided land share. Do not treat any such approval/event as an ownership transfer or assume every project requires every approval. Do not treat a later flat deed as proof that the underlying land-to-project-to-unit mapping is sound.
