Build the title chain for this case. Read every history document listed at the end with `read_document`, open title deeds only as the skill above allows, then give the result.

## Field rules

- Order: `title_chain` runs from the earliest transfer you reached to the latest
  sale. `chain_position` is 1, 2, 3 and so on, and the last link is the latest
  sale that fixed the target property. The application draws the chain graph
  from your links; do not write one.
- Links: every link is an ownership transfer of the target property and names
  who acquired it in `transferees`. A record you cannot connect to the target
  property is not a link.
- `continues_previous`: true when this link's transferors are the acquirers of
  the link before it. False for the first link and wherever the records leave a
  gap; then state the gap in this link's `summary_points`. A firm, company,
  association or trust is its partners, directors or trustees: before treating
  a switch between their individual names and the entity name as a gap, read the
  entity's constitution document and check whether they are the same people.
- `property_identity`: the target property only, as fixed from the latest sale.
- Identity: never output a transaction id. The application assigns ids and links
  between transfers when it saves.
- Evidence only: fill each field from what the cited document states and leave
  it null when the document does not state it. Record parties as the document
  writes them, one entry per named party; `transferors` may be empty when none
  is named. For conflicting OCR spellings or names, use the documentary
  reconciliation in the skill rather than copying the first occurrence.
  Never guess. Parties must agree with `summary_points`.
- Dates: `executed_date` is the date the document gives for the transfer, as
  written. When it gives a single date, put it in `executed_date`; use
  `registration_date` only when a separate registration date is stated.
- `transaction_number`: only an explicit registration or instrument number.
  Mutation numbers and notes such as "inheritance" do not belong here.
- `transaction_type`: a plain label for the act (Sale, Gift, Partition,
  Succession). Keep notes about where it was found out of it.
- Property fields: `property_schedule` is the schedule wording.
  `survey_number`, `plot_number`, `site_flat_number` and `cts_number` each hold
  only the bare identifier as written ("699A/15", "9", "G-04"), never a
  description, and never the same value in two fields.
- `summary_points`: plain-language sentences, one fact each, with no field
  labels such as "Seller:" and no filenames or document ids. State the parties
  and their roles, the property, the consideration when stated, and any
  conflict, gap or uncertainty. For a resolved OCR conflict, explain the
  conflicting reading and why the adopted identity is supported by the
  endorsements, signatures or matching recitals. Do not repeat the property identity.
- `source_refs`: the documents the link, event or identity detail was taken
  from. Cite only documents you read to the end, and copy each `doc_id` and
  `filename` from the lists below exactly.
- `evidence_status`: how the link is known, as the skill defines it. It must
  agree with `source_refs`: `corroborated` cites two or more documents,
  `register_reported` cites a history document, and `deed_reported` and
  `recital_reported` cite a title deed.
- `related_events`: the non-transfer entries (mortgage, release, lease,
  conversion, attachment) recorded during that link's ownership, with dates.
  It sits on the link, beside `documented_transaction`, not inside it.
- `missing_documents`: a top-level field only, always empty. Never put it on a
  chain link or inside documented_transaction. This phase reconstructs the chain from
  uploaded evidence; the verification stage handles document requests.
  Before marking a gap, fully read the relevant uploaded deeds and follow
  their recitals. When either party is a firm, company, association or trust,
  read its constitution document and check whether its members are the earlier
  acquirers; the same people trading under an entity name is not a gap. State
  remaining gaps or limited coverage in summary_points, without asking for uploads.
- Keep the result compact: no raw OCR, no long quotations.
