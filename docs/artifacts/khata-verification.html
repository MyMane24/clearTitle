How clearTitle verifies a Khata

In short: the Khata is the municipal tax register. We check it against the Sale Deed (what was bought) and the EC (registry history) and look for 4 things.

The 4 checks

  • Holder matches the buyer? If the khata is still in the seller’s name, the buyer’s mutation hasn’t happened yet — a gap.
  • Same property? PID / survey / CTS / locality must agree with the SD & EC. If not, the khata may belong to a different plot.
  • A-Khata or B-Khata? A authorised. B = unauthorised / pending violations → title at risk, must be flagged.
  • Tax paid up? Outstanding dues are shown as NEEDS_REVIEW — the buyer inherits them.

Verdicts

CLEAR TITLE
No unresolved mismatch, transfer, mortgage, or gap found anywhere in the three documents.
ATTENTION REQUIRED
Anything needs investigation — a B-Khata, unpaid mutation, mismatch, or missing record.

Anything missing is reported honestly

If a value is absent we say “Not found”, never guess. If the model can’t finish, the case shows a retry state and the PDF report stays locked — no fake green checks.

Bottom line for the reader: docs agree + A-Khata + tax paid + holder = buyer ⇒ clear. Anything else is surfaced as something to investigate — that’s the whole point.
