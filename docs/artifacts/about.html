About clearTitle

A title-verification platform for Karnataka property documents — it reads the Sale Deed, the Encumbrance Certificate (EC) and the Khata, and tells you, in plain language, whether the title is clear.

How it works

1. UploadSale Deed + EC + Khata (PDFs or images)
2. ReadDocuments are classified and extracted fields-to-field
3. CheckIdentifiers, chain, area, boundaries and khata are compared
4. VerdictClear title or attention required, with reasons
5. ReportA one-page PDF you can save or share

What it checks

  • Property match — survey/CTS/plot numbers and locality must agree on every document.
  • Ownership chain — a chronological ledger: who bought from whom, back to the original grant.
  • Area & boundaries — unit-aware: 9.14 x 12.19 m = 1200 sq ft = 111.48 sq m. Mismatched area or boundary gets flagged.
  • Khata status — A-Khata is authorised; B-Khata means unauthorised/pending violations. Is the buyer mutated as holder? Any tax dues?
  • Encumbrances — any mortgage or later sale after the deed date?

Verdicts

CLEAR TITLE — no unresolved mismatch, transfer, mortgage or gap.

ATTENTION REQUIRED — something needs investigation: a B-Khata, unpaid mutation, mismatch or missing record.

Nothing is ever guessed — if a value is absent the report says Not found.

Included

  • One-click self-service re-verification and title-chain rebuild.
  • A built-in demo property (DEMO-CLEAR-TITLE) showing a fully verified clear title, so you can see the result and report before running a real case.
